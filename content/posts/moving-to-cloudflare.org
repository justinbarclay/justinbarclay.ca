I've always loved free, no-fuss services for hosting my website. I started with Vercel before they were evil, then I moved to Netlify just as they were getting evil. Both services made publishing a barely active blog a breeze. They took care of building and deploying my Hugo blog and left me to focus on the part where I never write anything.

#+begin_quote
Having a blog is a lot like having a bookshelf; it's full of the hope of accomplishing more.
#+end_quote

Unfortunately, about a year after I moved to Netlify, they started going all-in on AI features.

#+NAME: Netlify 💘 AI
[[/images/2025-09-01_23-07-45_netlify-plus-ai-k-i-s-s-i-n-g.png]]

And as time has passed, they've only gone harder.

However, I do not enjoy AI, and I think its current success is built on profiting from [[https://jskfellows.stanford.edu/theft-is-not-fair-use-474e11f0d063][stolen work]]. This wouldn't be a problem for me if these models were open source and free to run, just like much of the work they were trained on. However, that isn't the case, and I want to make my work as difficult for AI to consume as possible.

#+begin_quote
Some may argue my work is already a poison pill for AI.
#+end_quote

On a recent(ish) episode of [[https://atp.fm/650][ATP]], they discussed [[https://www.cloudflare.com][Cloudflare's]] new anti-AI [[https://developers.cloudflare.com/bots/additional-configurations/ai-labyrinth/][crawling]] [[https://developers.cloudflare.com/bots/additional-configurations/ja3-ja4-fingerprint/][tools]].

While Netlify has its own guide on [[https://developers.netlify.com/guides/blocking-ai-bots-and-controlling-crawlers/][blocking AI]], it uses =robots.txt= and then recommends using =edge functions= to block them even more [[https://developers.netlify.com/guides/blocking-ai-bots-and-controlling-crawlers/#blocking-http-requests-based-on-the-user-agent-string-using-an-edge-function][harder]]. I don't want to have to run my own JS (or code in general); this is what I want to (not) pay someone else for.

#+begin_quote
I know that switching from one AI vendor to another is a token effort at best. However, I am happy to cut off my nose to spite my face, and I'll continue spiting my face until Cloudflare does something else egregious that forces me to rebel against them, too.
#+end_quote

** Moving from Netlify to Cloudflare
#+begin_quote
Current as of September 7th, 2025
#+end_quote
1. Make sure your Hugo config isn't 1.5 years out of date
2. Follow the multi-step setup instructions for setting up a [[https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/get-started/guide/][Cloudflare Worker]]
   #+begin_quote
   Don't fall for the Netlify to Cloudflare [[https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/migrations/migrating-from-netlify/][guide]], it's 3 years out of date.
   #+end_quote
3. Add Hugo-specific config for Hugo
   a) You could use Hugo's suggested [[https://gohugo.io/host-and-deploy/host-on-cloudflare/][setup]]
   b) You could throw caution to the wind and use the built-in Hugo for Cloudflare worker
   #+NAME: wrangler.toml
   #+begin_src toml
   # Configure Cloudflare Worker

   name = "hosting-cloudflare-worker"
   compatibility_date = "2025-07-31"

   [build]
   command = "hugo --gc --minify"

   [assets]
   directory = "./public"
   not_found_handling = "404-page"
   #+end_src
4. Add wrangler to your [[https://nix.dev/concepts/flakes.html][flake]]
   #+begin_src diff
   diff --git a/flake.nix b/flake.nix
   index 35bb12b..1bd297b 100644
   --- a/flake.nix
   +++ b/flake.nix
   @@ -72,7 +72,7 @@
            # Add dependencies that are only needed for development
            packages.blog = blog;
            devShells.default = pkgs.mkShell {
   -          packages = with pkgs; [ hugo go ];
   +          packages = with pkgs; [ hugo wrangler ];
            };
          }
        );
   #+end_src
5. Have wrangler get mad at you for naming your worker setup [[https://github.com/justinbarclay/justinbarclay.ca/pull/12][wrong]].
6. Switch your DNS over to Cloudflare from Netlify.
** Alternative
If I wanted to manage my own infrastructure, I could use [[https://github.com/TecharoHQ/anubis][Anubis]], but as I've already said, I want to manage _no infrastructure_ whatsoever.
